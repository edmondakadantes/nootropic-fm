Unlike static looping playlists or plain tone generators, nootropic.fm blends rich 48 kHz studio instrumentals with phase-coherent acoustic modulation tailored to your exact state, headphones or speakers, and environment.

HOW IT WORKS IN 30 SECONDS
1. Map Your Current State — Select how your mind feels right now on the interactive 2D Neural State Map (from scattered or wired to sluggish or overloaded).
