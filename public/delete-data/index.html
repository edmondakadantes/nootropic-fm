Delete Your Neural Pass & Session Data

You can permanently delete your nootropic.fm Neural Identity and all associated session records directly from this page—without reinstalling or opening the Android app.

What Data Is Deleted Immediately vs. Retained

  • Deleted immediately & permanently: Your profiles record (age/band, role, preferences, Sync Key hash, auth token hash), all linked sessions history (check-ins, ratings, reaction/tapping scores), and all personal bandit_stats learning weights.
  • Active Subscriptions: Deleting your app data does not automatically cancel an external Google Play subscription. If you have an active subscription on Google Play, cancel it in Google Play Store → Subscriptions so Google does not bill future renewals.
  • Statutory Accounting Records: If you previously completed a paid transaction, minimal non-behavioral payment invoice identifiers required by tax and accounting law are retained by the payment processor (Google Play or Stripe) for the statutory period.

Don't Have Your Sync Key?

  • If you still have the app or browser open: Tap Privacy, Credits & Delete My Data in the bottom bar and tap Delete My Identity & All Session Data (no Sync Key entry required on the active device).
  • Email Support: Contact support@nootropic.fm with any billing email or approximate creation date, and we will verify and process deletion within 7 business days.
