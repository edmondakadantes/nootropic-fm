Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · Applies to nootropic.fm Web & Android App (fm.nootropic.app)
Privacy Summary: nootropic.fm uses passwordless pseudonymous Neural Passes (NFM-XXXX-XXXX), zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero cross-app tracking, and zero sale of personal data. Listeners can erase their profile and all session records instantaneously in-app or at nootropic.fm/delete-data.

1. Audience & Non-Medical Wellness Scope (18+)

nootropic.fm is designed exclusively for adults aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children or minors under 18. nootropic.fm is a general cognitive wellness, focus, and relaxation audio application and is not a medical device.

2. Information We Collect & Why

We practice strict data minimization. Depending on how you use nootropic.fm, we process only the following categories of information:

Data Category Specific Fields Purpose & Optionality
Pseudonymous Neural Pass Random profile ID (anon_...), SHA-256 hashed Sync Key, age / age band (18+), optional display name, gender selection, and occupation/role category. Personalizes acoustic modulation priors (such as age-adjusted Alpha Hz priors and speaker/headphone safety) and cross-device Sync Key restoration. No password, phone number, or street address is collected.
Listening & Session Telemetry Selected mental state, target goal, audio genre, duration, optional reaction-time / rhythmic tapping check scores, and post-session rating. Adapts future psychoacoustic protocol recommendations to your profile. In EEA/UK/CH or when Global Privacy Control is active, database logging requires explicit opt-in; without consent, sessions run in 100% ephemeral local mode.
Subscription & Billing Metadata Plan tier (free / pro), subscription expiration timestamp, and payment processor reference IDs (Google Play Billing or Stripe on web, plus optional receipt email on web). Unlocks Pro audio entitlements. Payment card numbers are processed directly by Google Play or Stripe and never touch nootropic.fm servers.
Security & Rate-Limit Counters One-way cryptographic hashes of network IP / session nonces with short automatic expiration. Prevents automated abuse and protects real-time audio streaming infrastructure.

3. Permissions & Device Sensors

  • Foreground Media Playback (Android): Used solely to keep your chosen focus, calm, or sleep-winddown audio playing smoothly when your phone screen is locked, displaying standard media transport controls in your notification shade.
  • Zero Camera, Microphone, Contacts, or Location Access: nootropic.fm does not request microphone, camera, contacts, SMS, or GPS location permissions, and explicitly disables the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID).
  • Optional Bluetooth Heart-Rate Monitor (Web Bluetooth where supported): Only accessed if you explicitly click to pair an external BLE heart-rate strap during a session; never accessed in the background.

4. How We Share Information (Zero Ad Brokers)

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers or data brokers. Data is processed solely through essential infrastructure providers bound by strict security obligations:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — Encrypted edge hosting, D1 SQLite database, and R2 audio streaming storage (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest).
  • Google Play / Stripe — Subscription billing verification when you purchase a Pro pass.
  • Public Scientific Literature APIs (NCBI PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex) — Queried server-side by topic keyword only, without transmitting your profile identifier.

5. Data Retention & Instant Deletion

Local browser/app profile tokens automatically expire after 90 days of inactivity. You have the right to access, export, or permanently delete your Neural Pass and all associated session history at any time—without needing to reinstall the app:

  • In-App Instant Deletion: Open the app footer → Privacy, Credits & Delete My Data → tap Delete My Identity & All Session Data.
  • Web Self-Service Deletion (No App Required): Visit https://www.nootropic.fm/delete-data, enter your NFM-XXXX-XXXX Sync Key, and click Permanently Delete My Data. Deletion executes immediately across our live database tables (profiles, sessions, and personal bandit_stats).
  • Support-Assisted Deletion: If you lost your Sync Key or need billing record assistance, email support@nootropic.fm and we will process your request within 7 business days.

6. GDPR, UK GDPR & US State Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Residents of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, California, Virginia, Colorado, and other jurisdictions have the right to know what data is collected, request deletion, withdraw consent at any time, and be free from discrimination for exercising privacy rights. Because nootropic.fm does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" protections are active by default for every user.

7. Security

All network communication is encrypted via HTTPS/WSS (TLS 1.3). Authentication tokens and Sync Keys are stored exclusively as one-way SHA-256 cryptographic hashes in our database, and WebSocket audio tickets enforce atomic single-use nonces.

For privacy inquiries, data rights requests, or general support, contact us at: support@nootropic.fm.
