Physics 01 · Codec & ERB Masking

Lossy Playlist Compression vs. 48 kHz Float64

Pre-baked focus tracks distributed over standard lossy codecs (MP3, AAC, Bluetooth SBC) allocate bits using auditory masking curves. Because a cortical modulation sideband at -20 dBc to -26 dBc falls inside the same Equivalent Rectangular Bandwidth (ERB) as a loud synth pad or drum transient, encoders quantize it away. Open Speakers (~0.26 ms Notch) vs. Sealed In-Ear Buds
