# nootropic.fm — Suno Pro Studio Audio Prompt Pack

> **IMPORTANT IN SUNO PRO:**
> 1. Turn **Instrumental = ON** for EVERY track (zero lyrics, zero vocals, zero spoken samples).
> 2. Generate **3–4 tracks per genre** (~3–4 minutes each). Our Dual-Deck Crossfade engine seamlessly blends them into endless 40–120 minute sessions and fuses our **Multiband AM (>190 Hz) + Driving Safety EQ** directly into the music!
> 3. Download each track as `.mp3` and drop it into `audio-stems/<genre>/`, then run `npm run upload:stems` (or tell your AI assistant to upload them to Cloudflare R2).
